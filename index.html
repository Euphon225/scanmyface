FAQ

The things people ask first.

How does the face scan actually work?+
Your camera (or an uploaded photo) feeds a face-mesh model that runs in your browser. It locates 468 points on your face and converts the geometry into the slider values FC26 expects.
Do my photos stay private?+
Yes. The model runs on your device. No image, no landmark data, no scan output ever touches our servers.
Is it really 100% local?+
Yes. Open the Network tab while you scan — you'll see zero outbound requests. The PWA caches everything once and works offline after that.
Which games are supported right now?+
FC26 is fully supported with all 10 facial zones. UFC and WWE are in development — Premium users get them at no extra cost when they ship.
Do I need to download anything?+
No. It runs in your browser. Tap "Add to Home Screen" if you want a one-tap launcher, but no app-store install required.
Is Premium a one-time payment?+
Yes. $4.99 once. No subscription, no renewal, no email asking you to upgrade your plan.
